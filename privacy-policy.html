Introduction

Welcome to DBE Natural Sciences & Technology ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website at dbeducator.github.io.

This site provides free, CAPS-aligned educational resources for South African learners in Grades 5 to 12. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all visitors, especially learners and minors.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect any personal information from visitors. However, third-party services used on this site may collect certain data automatically:

  • IP address and general location
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search terms

This data is collected automatically by Google AdSense and is used for the purpose of serving relevant advertisements.

Google AdSense & Cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, visit the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

This website is designed for use by school learners, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Google AdSense ads served on this site are subject to Google's policies regarding child-directed content. We have configured our ad settings in compliance with applicable regulations including COPPA and POPIA.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

How We Use Information

Any data collected via third-party services is used solely for:

  • Displaying relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Analysing site traffic to improve educational content
  • Ensuring the security and performance of the website

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any visitor information to third parties beyond what is described in this policy.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external resources including YouTube videos and educational materials. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Your Rights (POPIA)

In accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is held about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
